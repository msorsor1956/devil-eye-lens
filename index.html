Good questions. Clear answers.

Is this a replacement headlight?

No. This product is decorative automotive ambient lighting. It does not replace required vehicle lighting.

What comes with each kit?

The single projector kit has a 55 cm cable. The twin projector kit has a 100 cm cable. A remote control is included; use the kit photos to compare the components.

Which eye colors are available?

Blue, green, purple and red are listed lighting modes. Preview imagery is illustrative; colors can look different in your installation.

Can I order online now?

Online ordering is not available yet. Pricing and payment options will appear when configured. You can choose to remember a kit selection on this device.

What about warranty and delivery?

The product record lists a one-year warranty. Shipping costs, delivery estimates, return terms and warranty conditions must be confirmed before payment. Package dimensions are 19 × 13 × 8 cm; these describe the box, not installation clearance.
