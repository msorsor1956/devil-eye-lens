NIGHT EYES / CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Privacy & storage

Updated October 6, 2026

This notice describes the current storefront operated by Sonnies Technology LLC, trading as Night Eyes / Devil Eye Light. PayPal checkout is available when its connection is active. Hosting-log and payment-record retention periods are still being finalized.

Browsing this website

The current storefront does not contain advertising pixels, analytics scripts, account registration or a contact form. Images, styles, scripts and video are served from this site except for PayPal payment components, which load when you select a PayPal-powered payment option. Apple Pay and Google Pay components also load when you select those options. The selected wallet provider and PayPal receive browser and device information to assess eligibility and operate the payment flow. The hosting provider necessarily receives technical request information such as your IP address, requested address and browser details to deliver the site and operate its infrastructure. Log retention is not yet confirmed.

Your kit, your choice

If you select “Remember my kit on this device,” this site stores the chosen color, kit and quantity in your browser under devil-eye-kit. The preference stays on your device; the selected kit, color and quantity are sent to our server when you calculate checkout or place an order. It stays until you clear it or your browser removes it. Unchecking the option removes the saved preference.

Payments

PayPal processes our card, PayPal, Venmo and supported wallet payments. We collect your name, email, shipping address and optional phone number in checkout to calculate totals and manage orders, shipping, refunds and support. We store the selected product, amount, payment references and status, customer and shipping details, and the version of the checkout policies accepted with your order. Your address and order amounts are sent to Stripe Tax to calculate applicable tax. They are sent to PayPal to process the order. Unpurchased checkout quotes expire after 15 minutes and are removed from our quote storage within approximately three hours. Customer details are not saved in browser local storage. Payment and order records are retained for fulfillment, accounting and legal obligations; a specific retention schedule is still being finalized. A secure, necessary checkout cookie associates your browser with your order for up to three hours. Card credentials are entered on PayPal and are not stored by this website. Stripe checkout is not yet enabled, but our server is configured to receive verified payment notifications from Stripe. It records event and checkout references, payment status, amount, currency, timestamps and available kit, color, quantity and order references to reconcile payments and avoid processing duplicate notifications. This receiver does not store card numbers, billing addresses or shipping addresses. Do not send card details by email. PayPal handles payment information under its own privacy statement.

Third-party services

This site runs on Railway. Technical information handled by the hosting service is subject to its applicable arrangements and privacy notice. No social media feeds or tracking embeds are loaded by the current storefront.

Privacy requests and updates

For privacy questions or requests concerning your information, email support@nighteyes.pro. This notice will be updated as ordering and related data-handling practices are introduced.
