Privacy & storage

Draft updated October 3, 2026

Draft notice for the current preview. The operator’s legal identity, privacy contact and hosting-log retention details must be confirmed before this becomes a final privacy policy.

Browsing this website

The current storefront does not contain advertising pixels, analytics scripts, account registration or a contact form. Images, styles, scripts and video are served from this site. The hosting provider necessarily receives technical request information such as your IP address, requested address and browser details to deliver the site and operate its infrastructure. Log retention is not yet confirmed.

Your kit, your choice

If you select “Remember my kit on this device,” this site stores the chosen color, kit and quantity in your browser under devil-eye-kit. This preference is not sent to a server by the storefront script. It stays until you clear it or your browser removes it. Unchecking the option removes the saved preference.

Payments

Online ordering is not enabled in this preview. When payments are introduced, this notice must describe the payment provider, order data, purposes, retention and customer rights. Do not send card details through this website or by email.

Third-party services

This site runs on Railway. Technical information handled by the hosting service is subject to its applicable arrangements and privacy notice. No social media feeds or tracking embeds are loaded by the current storefront.

Privacy requests and updates

A verified support and privacy contact must be published before launch. This draft does not claim compliance with every jurisdiction; the final notice must reflect the operator’s location, customers and actual practices.
